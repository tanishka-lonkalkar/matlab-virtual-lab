Aim

To study and understand the concept of double integration and evaluate integrals of functions of two variables.

Theory

Double integration is used to integrate functions of two variables.

General form: ∬ f(x,y) dA

It is used to calculate area, volume and physical quantities.

Simulation

Evaluate ∬(ax + by + c)
